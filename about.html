Our Story

A Platform Born From a Passion for Learning

MS Digital Skill Hub was born from a passion for learning, teaching, technology, and helping people develop practical skills that can create new opportunities.

Meet the Founder

Joy Thomas

Founder, MS Digital Skill Hub

Joy Thomas is a digital professional, educator, and lifelong learner whose journey has been shaped by curiosity, grit, and a commitment to continuous growth.

Her connection to education began at a very young age. Growing up with a mother who owned a school, Joy was surrounded by teaching and learning from childhood. She started teaching when she was just five years old, and what began as part of her upbringing gradually became a defining part of who she is.

Joy went on to study Microbiology at the Federal University of Technology, Minna (FUT Minna). While her academic background is in science, her curiosity continually pushed her beyond the boundaries of her degree and into new areas of learning.

In 2021, she began her journey as a Virtual Assistant. Much of her expertise was built through self-directed learning, research, practice, and a willingness to figure things out. She later joined ALX, further developing her digital and professional skills.

Her desire to understand technology from different perspectives also led her to study Product Management through the 3MTT programme, where she gained a deeper understanding of products, users, technology, and problem-solving.

Today, Joy continues to learn and evolve. She is currently exploring the world of engineering, with a particular focus on AI-native software engineering through Learn2Earn.

A Journey Driven by Curiosity

Joy's journey may look unconventional at first glance: Microbiology, Virtual Assistance, Product Management, teaching, and now AI-native software engineering.

But there is a common thread connecting every stage of her journey: curiosity.

She has always been curious to discover new things, understand how they work, and challenge herself to learn what she does not yet know. Her willingness to start from scratch and her grit to keep going have allowed her to continually reinvent herself and embrace new opportunities.

She believes that growth does not come from having everything figured out. It comes from being willing to learn, adapt, ask questions, and keep moving forward.

Why She Built MS Digital Skill Hub

For Joy, building MS Digital Skill Hub was a natural extension of a passion she has carried since childhood: teaching and helping people grow.

Having grown up around education and experienced the process of teaching herself new skills, she understands both the power of knowledge and the challenges that can come with starting something new.

MS Digital Skill Hub was created to provide a space where people can develop practical digital skills, discover new possibilities, and gain the confidence to pursue opportunities in an increasingly digital world.

Joy's vision is simple: people should have access to the knowledge and skills that can help them create better opportunities for themselves.

She is not only building a learning platform; she is building a community shaped by the same values that have guided her own journey: curiosity, grit, learning, and growth.

And her journey is far from over.

As she continues to explore new technologies, develop new skills, and learn about the future of work, Joy remains committed to one thing that has been part of her story since childhood:

Learning, teaching, and helping others grow.
